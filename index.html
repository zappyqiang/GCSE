GQGCSE Quest

Revise smarter. Score higher.

Short lessons, memory hooks and exam-style questions for GCSE Maths, Physics, Chemistry and Biology. Practise daily, find your weak topics, and sit timed mock exams before the real thing.

How it works

  • Work through each topic: read a short lesson with a memory hook, then answer questions to master the unit.
  • Questions you get wrong return on later days through spaced repetition, until they stick.
  • Use mock exams and the progress page to see which topics need work. Set your tier, science course and exam date in Settings.

Content follows the AQA specifications: Mathematics 8300, Physics 8463, Chemistry 8462 and Biology 8461. Other exam boards overlap heavily, but check your own board's list too. These games are revision practice, and are not made or endorsed by AQA.
