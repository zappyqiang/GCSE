GCSE Quest

Four cartoon worlds. Every knowledge point on the course, told as a comic, then fought as a game.

How to play

  • Pick a world and follow the trail. Each stop is a short comic, a memory trick and a monster to beat with the right answers.
  • Wrong answers go to the Mistake gym and come back on later days until they stick.
  • Set your tier and science course in each world's Settings. Progress saves automatically in this browser.

Content follows the AQA specifications: Mathematics 8300, Physics 8463, Chemistry 8462 and Biology 8461. Other exam boards overlap heavily, but check your own board's list too. These games are revision practice, and are not made or endorsed by AQA.
